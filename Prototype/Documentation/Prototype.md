# Creep Keepers — Beetleweight Undercutter (Fall 2025)
## Crimson Robotics (Washington State University)

A 3‑lb beetleweight undercutter combat robot developed in early Fall 2025 by  
**Gus Ballman, Carson Roskelley, Jayden Claytor, Natalie Massarenti**.

This prototype focused on a lightweight but rigid aluminum chassis, a TPU‑buffered weapon stack, and a hybrid PLA/TPU internal structure optimized for manufacturability and rapid iteration.

---

## Overview

| Subsystem | Material(s) | Mass |
|----------|--------------|------|
| **Chassis** | 6061 Aluminum | 1.25 lb |
| **Side & Internal Walls** | PLA | ~0.18 lb |
| **Weapon Stack** | Alloy Steel blade & bearing, TPU spacers, TPU/PLA O‑ring holder | ~0.39 lb (0.37 lb blade) |
| **Top & Back Armor** | TPU (single printed piece) | — |

---

## Chassis

**Material:** 6061 Aluminum  
**Mass:** 1.25 lb  

The chassis serves as the primary structural frame, providing rigidity for the undercutter weapon system and protection for internal components. Aluminum 6061 was selected for its balance of machinability, strength, and weight.

---

## Side & Internal Wall Assembly

**Material:** PLA  
**Mass:** ~0.18 lb  

These printed walls form the internal compartmentalization for electronics and help constrain the weapon stack and drivetrain. PLA was chosen for ease of printing and dimensional stability during prototyping.

---

## Weapon Stack

A compact, high‑inertia undercutter assembly designed around a steel blade and TPU damping elements.

**Components:**
+ **Blade:** AR 500 Steel  
+ **Needle Bearing:** Alloy Steel  
+ **Bottom Spacer:** PLA 
+ **Top O‑Ring Holder:** PLA  
+ **Total Mass:** 2.57 lb  
  - **Blade Mass:** 0.36 lb  

The TPU spacers and O‑ring holder provide vibration damping and help maintain axial alignment during high‑speed operation.

---

## Internal Organization

The internal layout prioritizes:
+ Clean separation of electronics and weapon drive  
+ Shock‑isolated mounting points  
+ Straightforward wiring paths  
+ Rapid serviceability between matches 

---

## Top & Back Armor Plate

**Material:** TPU  
**Manufacturing:** Printed as a single piece  

The TPU armor provides flexible impact absorption and reduces the likelihood of brittle failure during weapon‑to‑weapon engagements.
