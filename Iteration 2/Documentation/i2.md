# Creep Keepers — Beetleweight Undercutter  
## Iteration 2 (Spring 2026 → Completed April 2026)
## Nickname - "Chuckles"

+ Iteration 2 (Chuck;es) was the second competitive version following the first iteration used at the fall 2025 Crimson Robotics Bot Brawl. Developed by **Gus Ballman, Carson Roskelley, Jayden Claytor, and Natalie Massarenti**. This build focused on refining the chassis (removing material), improving internal layout, and improved weapon stack.
